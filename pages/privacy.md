This policy explains what data LeanMarketingStack collects and how it's used.

## Information we collect

- **Analytics:** we don't use any analytics or tracking scripts.
- **Email:** we don't run an email list and don't collect email addresses.
- **Cookies:** this site doesn't set its own cookies.
- **Outbound and affiliate links:** when you click a link to another company's site, that company may set cookies (for example, to credit a referral). Their privacy policy applies on their site.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub may collect technical information such as visitor IP addresses in server logs for security and to operate the service. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
