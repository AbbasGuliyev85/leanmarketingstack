LeanMarketingStack helps solo bloggers, creators and small business owners choose SEO and email marketing tools without overspending.

## What we do

We publish comparisons, "alternatives" guides and practical walkthroughs for budget-conscious site owners. We always tell you when a free tool is good enough.

## How we review

- We use official vendor pages and public information.
- We don't publish made-up prices, features or testimonials. When we can't confirm something, we say so and tell you to check it on the vendor's site.
- We update articles when tools change, and each article shows when it was last reviewed.

## Who's behind the site

LeanMarketingStack is an independent site. It isn't owned by or affiliated with any of the software companies it covers.
