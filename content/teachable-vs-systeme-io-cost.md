**Teachable** is one of the best-known names in online courses. **Systeme.io** ({{AFFILIATE:systeme-io}}) is an all-in-one tool for funnels, email and courses that has a free plan. If you're about to sell your first course, both can get you there. The question this guide answers is simple: **what will each one actually cost you, and what do you give up on the cheaper option?**

**How we checked:** every price, fee and limit below comes from the official pricing pages at teachable.com/pricing and systeme.io/pricing, both **checked on November 10, 2026**. Systeme.io's page said it was last updated in April 2026. We haven't run paid accounts on either platform, so we make no claims about video quality, support speed or email deliverability. Prices change often, so confirm them on each vendor's site before you buy.

## The short answer

- **Teachable has no free plan.** Its FAQ describes it as a paid service with a **7-day free trial** and a **30-day money-back guarantee**. Paid plans are **Starter** ($39 a month, or $29 a month billed yearly), **Builder** ($89 or $69) and **Growth** ($189 or $139). There's also a Custom plan for high-volume businesses, priced by sales call.
- **Teachable's Starter plan charges a 7.5% transaction fee** on your sales. Builder, Growth and Custom have 0% Teachable transaction fees when you use Teachable Payments, according to its FAQ.
- **Systeme.io has a free plan** that, according to its pricing page, never expires and needs no credit card. Paid plans cost **$17, $47 or $97 a month**, and every plan lists **0% transaction fees**.
- **Your own affiliate program** is listed from Teachable's Builder plan up. Systeme.io lists one on its free plan.

If you're starting from zero, Systeme.io can cost **$0** in year one. Teachable's cheapest option costs **$348** in year one on yearly billing, plus 7.5% of every sale.

What each plan adds, according to the same page:

Teachable's FAQ explains what a "product" is: any published course, coaching offer or digital download counts as one product, no matter how many videos or files it holds. **Memberships, bundles and unpublished drafts don't count** toward the limit. ## Transaction fees: where Teachable's Starter plan gets expensive

Every platform that takes card payments has processing fees. The real question is whether the platform takes its own cut on top.

**Teachable's FAQ** says only the Starter plan has a transaction fee, at **7.5%**, and that Builder, Growth and Custom have 0% transaction fees when you use Teachable Payments. It adds that standard payment processing fees may still apply. The pricing page we checked didn't list the exact processing rate, so we leave it out of the math below.

**Systeme.io's pricing page** lists no transaction fees on any plan and says you can accept payments with Stripe or PayPal at no extra cost. Your payment processor still charges its own fee.

Here's an example with **100 sales of a $50 course, or $5,000 in revenue**, counting only the platform's own fee and plan price:

| Setup | Plan cost (1 year) | Platform fee | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Systeme.io Free | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Systeme.io Startup | $204 | $0 | $204 |
| Teachable Starter (yearly) | $348 | $375 (7.5% of $5,000) | $723 |
| Teachable Builder (yearly) | $828 | $0 | $828 |

On Teachable, the 7.5% fee decides when upgrading makes sense. Builder costs $480 a year more than Starter on yearly billing, and 7.5% of **$6,400** is $480. So by our math, once your yearly course sales pass roughly $6,400, Builder becomes cheaper than staying on Starter. On monthly billing the gap is $600 a year, which you'd reach at about $8,000 in sales.

On Systeme.io, the fee question doesn't come up: you move up a plan only when you need more contacts, funnels or courses.

## Where Teachable is stronger

Teachable isn't just a pricier version of the same thing. Based on its pricing page:

- **Built for courses first.** Teachable is focused on courses, coaching and digital downloads. Systeme.io is built around sales funnels, email and automation, with courses as one part of it. If you want the course experience to be the center of everything, that focus can feel simpler.
- **Student mobile apps.** Starter lists iOS and Android student apps. Systeme.io's pricing page didn't list a dedicated student app when we checked.
- **Global payments and taxes** are listed on Starter, and the Custom plan adds automated international tax handling. Systeme.io's pricing page didn't list an equivalent sales tax feature when we checked, so ask them if you sell to buyers in many countries.
- **More products on cheaper paid plans.** Starter allows 5 products, and memberships and bundles don't count toward the limit. Systeme.io Free allows 1 course and Startup 5.
- **Course certificates** are listed on Growth.
- **A 30-day money-back guarantee.** The FAQ says you can request a refund within your first 30 days.

## Common questions

### Does Teachable have a free plan?

No. Teachable's FAQ says it's a paid service with a 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Its cheapest plan, Starter, cost $29 a month billed yearly or $39 a month billed monthly when we checked.

### Does Teachable take a cut of my sales?

On the Starter plan, yes: 7.5% per sale. Builder, Growth and Custom have 0% Teachable transaction fees when you use Teachable Payments. Standard payment processing fees may still apply on every plan.

### Is Systeme.io cheaper than Teachable?

At every level we compared, yes. Systeme.io's free plan costs nothing, its paid plans run $17 to $97 a month, and none charge a platform transaction fee. Teachable starts at $29 a month on yearly billing with a 7.5% fee, and its first no-fee plan is $69 a month billed yearly.

### What counts as a product on Teachable?

According to Teachable's FAQ, each published course, coaching offer or digital download counts as one product. Memberships, bundles and unpublished drafts don't count.

### Can I get a refund from Teachable?

Its FAQ says you can request a refund within your first 30 days under its money-back guarantee. You can cancel any time, and cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
