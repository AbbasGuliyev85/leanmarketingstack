**Podia** and **Systeme.io** ({{AFFILIATE:systeme-io}}) both promise the same thing to beginners: one place to sell online courses, digital downloads and coaching, with a website, checkout and email built in. If you're choosing between them, the features overlap more than you might expect. The prices don't.

This guide answers one practical question: **what will each platform actually cost you, and where do the limits bite first?**

**How we checked:** every price, fee and limit below comes from the official pricing pages at podia.com/pricing and systeme.io/pricing, both **checked on November 3, 2026**. The Systeme.io page said it was last updated in April 2026. We haven't run paid accounts on either platform, so we don't make claims about page speed, support quality or email deliverability. Prices change, so confirm them on each vendor's site before you buy.

## The short answer

- **Systeme.io** has a free plan that never expires and needs no credit card, according to its pricing page. Paid plans cost $17, $47 or $97 a month.
- **Podia** has no free plan. It offers a **14-day free trial** with no credit card, then three paid plans: **Mover** ($49 a month, or $42 a month billed annually), **Shaker** ($99 or $84) and **Earthquaker** ($179 or $150).
- Podia's cheapest plan charges a **5% transaction fee** on top of card processing. Systeme.io lists **0% transaction fees** on every plan, including Free.
- The biggest gap most beginners won't notice at first: Podia's plans include only **100 to 1,000 email subscribers**, while Systeme.io's free plan allows **2,000 contacts** with unlimited email sends.

If you're starting from zero, Systeme.io can cost you nothing in year one. Podia costs at least **$504** in year one on its cheapest annual plan, before transaction fees.

## Transaction fees: the cost that grows with your sales

Neither platform is free to sell through, because card processors always take a cut. The question is whether the platform adds its own fee on top.

**Podia's FAQ** says the Mover plan carries a **5% transaction fee**, and that Shaker and Earthquaker carry no Podia fee. Separately, your processor (Stripe or PayPal) charges what Podia's page describes as a standard fee of **2.9% + 30¢**, which may vary by location.

**Systeme.io's pricing page** lists 0% transaction fees and says you can accept payments through Stripe or PayPal at no extra cost. You still pay the processor's own fee.

Here's a simple example: **100 sales of a $50 product ($5,000 in revenue)**, using the 2.9% + 30¢ processor rate Podia quotes.

| Setup | Processor fees | Platform fee | Total fees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Systeme.io (any plan) + Stripe | $175 | $0 | $175 |
| Podia Mover + Stripe | $175 | $250 (5% of $5,000) | $425 |
| Podia Shaker + Stripe | $175 | $0 | $175 |

This is our arithmetic using listed rates, not a quote. At $5,000 in sales, Podia Mover's 5% fee costs you $250, which is close to the $504 yearly difference between Mover and Shaker on annual billing. Once your yearly sales on Mover pass roughly $10,000, the 5% fee alone ($500) is about what upgrading to Shaker costs extra per year. Below that, Mover plus the fee is usually still cheaper than Shaker.

## The email limit most people miss

This is the single most important difference for a growing creator.

According to Podia's FAQ, every Podia plan includes Podia Email "free up to a certain number of subscribers": **100 on Mover, 500 on Shaker and 1,000 on Earthquaker**. If your list is bigger, you can add more subscribers, and Podia says it emails you as you get close and never auto-upgrades you. The pricing page we checked didn't list the price of extra subscribers, so check that inside the account before you plan around it.

Compare that with **Systeme.io's free plan: 2,000 contacts and unlimited email sends**, according to its pricing page. Startup raises that to 5,000 contacts for $17 a month.

In plain terms, a creator with a 1,500-person email list would exceed even Podia's top plan's included subscribers, while staying inside Systeme.io's free plan. If email marketing is how you plan to sell, this matters more than the headline price. ## Where Podia is stronger

To be fair to Podia, it isn't just a pricier version of the same thing. Based on its pricing page:

- **Sales tax handling for 230 countries and 20+ currencies** are listed on every plan. If you sell to buyers in many countries, that's useful to have built in. Systeme.io's pricing page didn't list an equivalent sales tax feature when we checked, so confirm with them if you need it.
- **Coaching, events and community** are listed as standard on every Podia plan, with up to 25 community spaces even on Mover.
- **Higher product limits.** Mover allows 50 products and Shaker 150, compared with 1 course on Systeme.io Free and 5 on Startup.
- **Migration help** on every plan. Podia's FAQ says it will move up to 50 products on Mover and up to 150 on Shaker and Earthquaker, including course content, downloads and customer emails.
- **Email and chat support** on every plan.

If you're already earning and have many small products to sell worldwide, Podia's design around a simple storefront may suit you. Systeme.io, by contrast, is built around sales funnels and automations, which some people find more powerful and others find more to learn.

## Where Systeme.io is stronger

- **A free plan that doesn't expire**, while Podia's free access ends after 14 days.
- **No platform transaction fee on any plan**, including Free.
- **Much larger email limits**: 2,000 contacts free versus 100 on Podia Mover.
- **An affiliate program on every plan**, while Podia lists it only on Shaker and above.
- **Sales funnels with upsells and order bumps.** Systeme.io's plan comparison table includes rows for 1-click upsells and order bumps, though the table didn't load per-plan values when we checked, so confirm which plan you need. Podia lists upsells only on Shaker and above.
- **Gentle downgrades.** Systeme.io's FAQ says that if you cancel a paid plan, your account reverts to the free plan at the next billing date instead of being shut down.

The free plan has real limits, though: **1 automation rule, 1 email campaign, 3 funnels and 1 course**. And one detail to watch: the free plan FAQ lists **500 students**, while another part of the same pricing page suggests other platforms limit students and Systeme.io doesn't. We're reporting both as written. If student count matters to you, confirm it inside the account before you launch.

## Refunds and trials

**Podia** gives you 14 days free with no card. After you pay, its FAQ says you can request a refund within **72 hours of your most recent payment**, and it doesn't do partial refunds on annual plans. So if you buy a year up front, be sure first.

**Systeme.io** doesn't need a trial in the same way, because the free plan stays free. You can build your whole product on it and only pay when you hit a limit.

## Who should pick which

**Pick Systeme.io if:**

- You haven't made your first sale yet.
- You want to grow an email list past a few hundred people without paying for it.
- You want your own affiliate program without moving to an $84-to-$99-a-month plan.
- You want to keep every cent of your sales except the card processor's fee.

**Consider Podia if:**

- You sell many digital products, coaching or events to buyers in different countries and want sales tax handled for you.
- Your email list is small, or you already use a separate email tool.
- You prefer a simple storefront over sales funnels.
- Your sales are high enough that $84 to $99 a month for Shaker is a small share of revenue.

## Common questions

### Does Podia have a free plan?

No. When we checked, Podia offered a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, then paid plans starting at $42 a month billed annually or $49 a month billed monthly. Systeme.io does have a free plan that, according to its pricing page, never expires.

### Is Systeme.io cheaper than Podia?

At every level we compared, yes. Systeme.io's paid plans run $17 to $97 a month, and its top plan costs less than Podia's Shaker plan on monthly billing. Systeme.io also lists no platform transaction fee, while Podia's Mover plan charges 5%.

### Does Podia take a cut of my sales?

On the Mover plan, Podia's FAQ says it charges a 5% transaction fee. Shaker and Earthquaker have no Podia fee. Your payment processor's own fee applies on every plan.

### How many email subscribers does Podia include?

According to its FAQ, 100 on Mover, 500 on Shaker and 1,000 on Earthquaker, with the option to add more. Systeme.io's free plan includes 2,000 contacts.

### Can I get a refund from Podia?

Podia's FAQ says to message them within 72 hours of your most recent payment for a refund, and that it doesn't give partial refunds on annual plans.
