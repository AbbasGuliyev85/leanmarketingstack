**Kartra** and **Systeme.io** ({{AFFILIATE:systeme-io}}) are both sold as all-in-one platforms. Each one bundles landing pages, sales funnels, email marketing, checkouts and memberships or courses, so you don't have to stitch five tools together. They're priced very differently, though. This guide answers the question a beginner on a budget actually has: **what will each one cost me in the first year, and what do I get or give up for that money?**

**How we checked:** every price, limit and fee below comes from the two official pricing pages, [kartra.com/pricing](https://kartra.com/pricing) and [systeme.io/pricing](https://systeme.io/pricing). We **checked both on November 24, 2026**. Systeme.io's page said it was last updated in April 2026. We haven't run paid accounts on either platform, so we make no claims about deliverability, page speed, support quality or ease of use. Where a page contradicted itself or we couldn't read a figure clearly, we say so instead of guessing. Prices change, so confirm them on each vendor's own site before you pay.

## The short answer

- **Kartra has no free plan.** Its pricing page offers a **14-day free trial** ("Try any plan of Kartra for free") and a **30-day money-back guarantee**. Paid plans are **Essentials** ($59 a month, or $52 a month billed annually), **Starter** ($119 or $99), **Growth** ($229 or $189) and **Professional** ($549 or $429). The yearly toggle says you can "save up to 22%".
- **Systeme.io has a free plan.** According to its pricing page, it has no end date and needs no credit card. Paid plans cost **$17, $47 or $97 a month**, and every plan lists **0% transaction fees**.
- **Kartra's cheapest plan charges a 5% transaction fee** on your sales and covers only **500 contacts**, 5 pages, 1 product and 1 membership or course. Kartra's other plans list 0% transaction fees.
- **Running your own affiliate program** is included on every Systeme.io plan, free included. On Kartra, affiliates first appear on **Growth**, at $189 to $229 a month.

If you're starting from zero, Systeme.io can cost **$0** in year one. Kartra's cheapest option costs about **$624 a year** on annual billing, before its 5% fee on sales. Its first plan without a transaction fee costs about **$1,188 a year**.

For comparison, the Systeme.io free plan lists no transaction fee and processes up to $10,000 in total sales. A beginner who sells less than that could stay on the free plan. Once you pass it, Startup costs $17 a month.

## Where Kartra gives you more

- **Built-in helpdesk** tools (support tickets from Growth, live chat on Professional), which Systeme.io's pricing page doesn't list.

- **Kartra AI** copywriting credits on every plan (30 uses on Essentials, 100 on Starter).
- **HIPAA compliance at no extra cost**, according to the FAQ, which may matter to health and wellness coaches who collect sensitive information.
- **Checkout language options**: the FAQ lists about two dozen checkout languages and 21 account currencies.

If you run an established coaching business with a large list, a support team and complex funnels, some of these could replace other paid tools. That makes Kartra's price easier to justify. ## Which one should you pick?

**Choose Systeme.io if:**

- You're starting out and want to test an offer for $0.
- Your list is under 10,000 contacts and your budget is under $50 a month.
- You want to run your own affiliate program without paying for a top-tier plan.
- You don't want a percentage of your sales going to your software.

**Consider Kartra if:**

- You already earn enough that $99 to $189 a month is easy to justify.
- You want video hosting, a helpdesk and funnel simulation in the same account as your pages and email.
- HIPAA compliance or multi-currency checkouts are must-haves for your business.
- You plan to use the 14-day trial to confirm Kartra's tools replace other software you pay for today.

## FAQ

### Is Kartra free?

No. Kartra's pricing page shows no free plan. It offers a 14-day free trial on any plan and a 30-day money-back guarantee. The cheapest paid plan, Essentials, costs $59 a month, or $52 a month billed annually, plus a 5% fee on sales.

### Is Systeme.io cheaper than Kartra?

At every contact level we compared, yes. Systeme.io's paid plans run $17 to $97 a month. Kartra's run $52 to $549 a month, depending on plan and billing. Even Systeme.io's top Unlimited plan ($97 a month, unlimited contacts) costs less than Kartra Starter on monthly billing ($119 a month, 2,500 contacts).

### Does Kartra charge transaction fees?

Only on Essentials. The FAQ says, "The Essentials plan includes a 5% transaction fee, while our Starter, Growth, Professional, and Enterprise plans have no additional fees." Systeme.io lists 0% transaction fees on all plans. You still pay your payment processor's card fees on both platforms.

### What happens if I go over my contact limit?

On Kartra, the FAQ says you're automatically upgraded to the next tier and charged a mid-cycle reconciliation fee. Systeme.io's pricing page tells you to choose a plan based on your contact count and says you can upgrade or cancel at any time. If you cancel a paid plan, your account reverts to the free plan at the end of the billing period.

### Can I switch from Kartra to Systeme.io?

Systeme.io's pricing page lists "free migration" as a perk. We haven't tested it, so check with Systeme.io support about what it covers before you count on it.
