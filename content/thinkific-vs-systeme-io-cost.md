## The short answer

- **Thinkific has no free plan.** Its pricing FAQ says that instead, every account starts with a **30-day free trial**. Paid plans are **Start** ($109 a month, or $82 a month billed annually), **Grow** ($219 or $164) and **Expand** ($499 or $374). A Plus tier for enterprises is priced through sales.
- **Systeme.io has a free plan** that, according to its pricing page, never expires and needs no credit card. Paid plans cost **$17, $47 or $97 a month**, and every plan lists **no transaction fees**.
- **Thinkific gives you unlimited courses on every plan.** Systeme.io limits courses by plan: 1 on Free, 5 on Startup, 20 on Webinar and unlimited on Unlimited.
- **Systeme.io includes email marketing to your whole list, sales funnels and your own affiliate program, even on the free plan.** Thinkific lists email automation and affiliate reporting on every plan, but bulk email to students starts on Grow.

If you're starting from zero, Systeme.io can cost **$0** in year one. Thinkific's cheapest paid option costs roughly **$980 a year** on annual billing, or **$1,308** if you pay month to month.

## Payment fees: what each page actually says

Every platform that takes card payments has processing fees. The question is whether the platform adds its own cut.

**Thinkific's FAQ** says its built-in processor, **Thinkific Payments**, charges standard processing fees "in line with other processors like Stripe and PayPal at 2.9%". If you use Stripe or PayPal instead, it says an **additional third-party payment gateway fee applies, and it varies by plan type**, in select countries. It also says Apple Pay and Google Pay fees vary, and that sellers on Thinkific Payments don't pay that gateway fee. The pricing page didn't list a separate percentage platform fee on top of Thinkific Payments.

**Systeme.io's pricing page** lists no transaction fees on any plan and says you can accept payments with Stripe or PayPal at no extra cost. Your payment processor still charges its own fee.

In practice: on Thinkific, use Thinkific Payments if it's available in your country to avoid the extra gateway fee. On Systeme.io, connect Stripe or PayPal and you only pay the processor's own rate.

## Common questions

### Does Thinkific have a free plan?

No. Thinkific's FAQ says it doesn't have a free plan and instead offers a 30-day free trial. You can cancel Start, Grow or Expand at any time, including during the trial, according to the same FAQ.

### How much is Thinkific per month?

When we checked, Start cost $109 a month billed monthly or $82 a month billed annually, Grow cost $219 or $164, and Expand cost $499 or $374. Plus is priced through sales.

### Does Thinkific charge transaction fees?

Its FAQ says Thinkific Payments charges standard processing at 2.9%, in line with Stripe and PayPal. Using Stripe or PayPal directly adds a third-party gateway fee that varies by plan in select countries.

### Is Systeme.io cheaper than Thinkific?

For most beginners, yes. Systeme.io's free plan costs nothing, and its paid plans run $17 to $97 a month. Thinkific starts at $82 a month on annual billing. The exception is a creator with more than 20 courses: Systeme.io would need its $97-a-month Unlimited plan, which costs more than Thinkific Start billed annually.

### Can I move from Systeme.io to Thinkific later?

You keep ownership of your course content either way, so you can rebuild it elsewhere. Systeme.io's pricing page also lists "free migration" as a perk on its plans. Check each platform's help center for export options before you commit.
