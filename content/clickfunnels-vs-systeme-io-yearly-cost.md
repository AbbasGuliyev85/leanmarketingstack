Monthly prices are designed to look small. A $97 bill doesn't feel like much in one month, but over a year it's more than a thousand dollars, and over three years it can be the biggest software cost a small online business has.

This article puts **ClickFunnels** and **Systeme.io** ({{AFFILIATE:systeme-io}}) side by side and does the yearly math with the numbers shown. Both are all-in-one platforms for sales funnels, email, courses and checkout, so they compete for the same customer: the creator, coach or small business that wants to sell online without stitching tools together.

**How we checked:** all prices and limits come from the official pricing pages at **systeme.io/pricing** and **clickfunnels.com/pricing**, both **checked on October 22, 2026**. We show our arithmetic so you can redo it with current prices. Prices change, so always confirm current details on each vendor's site before you buy. We don't cover features we haven't verified on those pages.

## Year-one cost: the straight math

Here's each plan multiplied out over 12 months.

**ClickFunnels**

- Launch: $97 × 12 = **$1,164/year** billed monthly, or about **$970/year** billed annually ($1,164 − $194)
- Scale: $197 × 12 = **$2,364/year** monthly, or about **$1,970/year** annually
- Optimize: $297 × 12 = **$3,564/year** monthly, or about **$2,970/year** annually
- Dominate: **$5,997/year** (annual only)

**Systeme.io (monthly billing)**

- Free: **$0/year**
- Startup: $17 × 12 = **$204/year**
- Webinar: $47 × 12 = **$564/year**
- Unlimited: $97 × 12 = **$1,164/year**

Notice the last line. Systeme.io's top plan, with unlimited contacts, costs the same per year as ClickFunnels' entry plan billed monthly: $1,164.

## What the math doesn't capture

Price isn't everything, and an honest comparison has to say where ClickFunnels might justify its cost for some people.

- **Team members and workspaces.** ClickFunnels lists team members and workspaces (sub-accounts) by plan, from 2 team members on Launch up to 20 on Dominate. Systeme.io says every plan, including free, allows unlimited sub-accounts, each with the same limits as your main plan. If you manage several brands, compare the details on both pages.
- **Email caps.** ClickFunnels lists a monthly email cap on each plan (50K a month on Launch). Systeme.io lists unlimited email sends on all plans. If you email a large list often, this matters.
- **Extras on ClickFunnels' pricing page.** It lists AI tool word allowances, onboarding calls, a training library and, on higher plans, priority and live chat support, a custom code editor and API access. If you'd actually use those, factor them in. If you wouldn't, they're not worth paying for.
- **Ecosystem and training.** ClickFunnels is well known for its training and community around funnel building. Some people value that a lot. That's a personal judgment, not something a pricing page can settle.
- **Ease of use.** We haven't benchmarked either editor. Try both before committing.

## Hidden costs to budget for on either platform

Whichever tool you pick, the subscription isn't your only cost. Budget for these too:

- **Payment processing fees** from Stripe or PayPal on every sale. Neither platform removes them.
- **A domain name**, if you want your own address. Both platforms support custom domains.
- **Your time.** Rebuilding funnels later is the real switching cost, so pick a tool you can live with for a couple of years.

None of these changes the comparison much, because they apply equally to both tools.

## Common questions

### Is Systeme.io really cheaper than ClickFunnels?

At every list size we checked, Systeme.io's published prices were lower. The biggest gap is under 2,000 contacts, where Systeme.io's free plan costs $0 and ClickFunnels' cheapest plan was about $970 a year on annual billing.

### Can I switch from ClickFunnels to Systeme.io later?

Systeme.io's pricing page advertises "free migration". Check what that covers before you rely on it.
